## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-17

### Added

- Dims list items that Zillow marks as advertising slots, and cards labelled Sponsored, Promoted or Featured, on zillow.com search results.
- A small "Sponsored — show" button on each dimmed card shows it at full strength, and clicking it again dims it.
- A toolbar badge counts the cards dimmed on the current page.
- One on/off switch in the popup; turning it off shows every card normally.
- Makes no network requests and reads no listing details.
- Needs a single permission, `storage`, plus access to zillow.com pages.
- Not affiliated with or endorsed by Zillow Group.
