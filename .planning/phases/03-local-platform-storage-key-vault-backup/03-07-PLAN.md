<objective>
Add the optional passphrase lock to the vault using envelope encryption: a PBKDF2-SHA256 (600,000 iterations, 16-byte salt) key-encryption key wraps a random AES-GCM-256 data key (DEK); each remembered key is sealed under the DEK with a fresh 12-byte IV and provider-bound AAD. Includes lock/unlock, change/remove passphrase, the persisted 5-attempt doubling cooldown, and cross-tab lock.

Purpose: PLAT-03 ("lock remembered keys behind a passphrase"; success criterion 2: after reopening, keys stay unusable until the passphrase is entered). Envelope encryption (planner's discretion within CONTEXT's PBKDF2 + AES-GCM decision) is what lets a reload of the same tab stay unlocked — matching the approved copy "unlocked until you close this tab or lock them" — while a new tab or session is locked, and lets a passphrase change rewrap one key instead of re-encrypting every row.
Output: `lock.ts`, Vault lock methods, `vault-lock.test.ts` with mutation proofs.
</objective>
