# Phase 9 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for frontend phases. Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, verified by gsd-ui-checker.
> Design intelligence: UI/UX Pro Max (`src/ui-ux-pro-max/scripts/search.py --domain ux` for "AI content generator multiple variants copy button", "copy clipboard feedback", "print PDF report layout", "form progressive disclosure steps", "card grid responsive comparison", "data table print"; `--domain product` for "real estate marketing content studio agent"; run 2026-10-04). **Adopted:** realistic sample content instead of placeholder text (every tool has a "Fill with sample facts" path), a visible confirmation after every copy (never a silent success), loading then success/error feedback on every submit, reserved space for async content so streaming and loading cause no layout shift, horizontal-scroll wrappers or card layouts for wide tables at 375px, and back navigation that keeps browser history (`?id=` written with `replaceState`, real links between tools). **Rejected:** the product search's "Trust Blue + Gold, Glassmorphism" and "Brutalism + bold brand colors" output (they conflict with the locked Phase 2 tokens); thumbs up/down AI feedback (no backend, and Phase 5 locked the exits); truncate-with-ellipsis for generated posts (a cut post can drop the disclosure or cut a claim mid-sentence, so over-limit posts are gated instead). **No new hex values.**

**Sources legend:** `[CTX]` 09-CONTEXT.md (locked), `[RES]` 09-RESEARCH.md, `[P2]` approved 02-UI-SPEC.md, `[P3]` approved 03-UI-SPEC.md, `[P4]` approved 04-UI-SPEC.md, `[P5]` approved 05-UI-SPEC.md, `[P6]` approved 06-UI-SPEC.md, `[P7]` approved 07-UI-SPEC.md, `[P8 draft]` 08-UI-SPEC.md (draft under verification), `[REQ]` REQUIREMENTS.md / ROADMAP.md (STU-01..04), `[STACK]` CLAUDE.md, `[SKILL]` UI/UX Pro Max, `[D]` researcher discretion (CONTEXT "Claude's Discretion", or a UI detail upstream documents don't cover).

**Honesty rules for this phase (apply to every string below):**
1. **Code owns every compliance-critical string.** The disclosure, license number, beat timings, hashtag caps, the market-information label, and the Equal Housing Opportunity statement are written by Tran's code, never by the model `[RES]`.
2. **No invented numbers or features.** Every numeral the model writes must come from what the agent typed (listing, social, reel) or from figures Tran computed (snapshot). Anything else is a **Must fix** finding `[CTX]` `[P7 number check]`.
3. **Results in the reel are the agent's own.** An empty results field produces the literal placeholder `[Add a real result you can document]`, never a made-up figure `[CTX]`.
4. **The Market Snapshot is market information only.** It is never called, or described as, a comparative market analysis or a professional valuation. The words "appraisal", "appraise", "CMA", "worth" (for a home), "guarantee", and "testimonial" never appear in Phase 9 static copy (see the label decision in Copywriting).
5. **Other agents' listings are never advertised.** The optional listings section shows counts, price ranges, and medians only: no addresses, photos, or listing-agent names `[RES Pitfall 8]`.
6. **Nothing is posted or sent by Tran.** Content leaves the browser only when the agent copies it, opens it in their email app, or prints it `[P5]`.
7. **Sample is unmistakable.** Sample facts, sample output, sample market data and the sample agent profile always carry the Phase 4 "Sample data" chip, on screen and in print `[P4]`.

## Typography

Reused: exactly **4 sizes** (14 / 16 / 24 / 40) and **2 weights** (400, 600) `[P2]`. Phase 9 introduces no size and no weight. It reuses Phase 7's **Figure** role (Inter, tabular, 24px) for snapshot key figures `[P7]`.

| Body | Inter | 16px | 400 (form text, generated text, table cells, checklist items, EHO sentence) / 600 (field labels, legends inside cards, button labels, platform card titles `h3`, tool card titles `h2`, print section `h2`s, brokerage name in the print contact block) | 1.6 | Everything else, including all generated copy |

| Display | Fraunces | 40px | 600 | 1.1 | The single screen `h1` per studio route ("Studio", "Listing description", "Social post pack", "Reel script", "Market Snapshot") |

Rules:
- Generated text is Body 16/400, line-height 1.6, `white-space: pre-wrap`, `overflow-wrap: anywhere`, never Fraunces `[P5]`. Hashtags render inline in that text (no chip styling), so the copied text and the displayed text are identical.
- Character counts, beat times ("0:00 to 0:03"), costs, ZIP codes, license numbers and every snapshot figure use `tabular-nums` `[P7]`.
- **Print prominence rule** `[RES §A.4]`: the brokerage name renders at a size **≥** the agent or team name everywhere it appears in the report (band: brokerage Heading 24/600 over agent Body 16/600; contact block: brokerage Body 16/600 over agent Body 16/400). The license number is never smaller than 14px, which is the smallest text Phase 9 prints. A Playwright computed-style test asserts both.
- Platform card titles are `h3` styled as Body 16/600 so three cards don't stack Fraunces `[P8 board column rule]`.
- `ChartFigure` titles on the print route render as `h2` in Body 16/600 (via the `print` prop) to match the print section hierarchy `[P7]` `[D]`.
- No text below 14px. No Fraunces below 24px `[P3]`.

## Copywriting Contract

Voice `[P2]` `[P3]`: plain, warm, specific, second person, short sentences. Every string passes the Phase 2 banned-terms grep **with no allowlist** `[P2]`. ### Report label decision `[D]` (resolves Phase 7 Planner Note 5 and RES Pitfall 10)

CONTEXT's working label is "Market information only — not a CMA or appraisal". Both "CMA" (Phase 7 honesty rule) and "appraisal" (Phase 2 grep) are banned in static copy, and Phase 2 forbids an allowlist. The research proposed a `NEGATED_ALLOW` exception; **this contract rejects it** and words the label without either term, following Phase 2's precedent of "not professional valuations" in place of "not appraisals". The label keeps CONTEXT's meaning: it states that the report is market information and is neither a comparative market analysis nor a valuation. The E2E asserts the strings below (not CONTEXT's working phrase).

- **Short label** (print header, builder lead area): "Market information only. Not a comparative market analysis or a professional valuation of any property."
- **Full label** (`disclaimerFull`, print **Important** block): "This report summarizes listing-based market statistics from RentCast as of {Mon D, YYYY}. It is market information only. It is not a comparative market analysis, a professional valuation of any property, or investment, legal, or tax advice. Figures are estimates from listings data, may not include every property, and can be wrong. {Notes sentence}" where the notes sentence (only when Market notes are included) is "The market notes were drafted with AI assistance from the figures in this report and reviewed by {Agent name}."

## Data & Privacy UI Rules

- Studio inputs contain no client PII by design; `containsClientData` is false for every studio prompt, so the Gemini dialog does not apply `[P5]`. If an agent types a client's name into a free-text field, Phase 5 redaction still runs and "What will be sent" shows it `[P5]`.
- The disclosure, footer, checklist, hashtag caps and timings are appended or computed locally and never sent to the AI provider; "What will be sent" adds the line "Your disclosure is added in this browser after the draft is written. It isn't sent to {Provider}." `[P8 draft]`
- The snapshot record freezes metrics, the aggregated listings summary (whitelisted keys only), the reviewed narrative, options, provenance and a copy of the disclosure fields at save time; it never stores RentCast raw responses or any listing address `[RES]`.
- Logo blobs render only through `<img src="blob:…">` (CSP `img-src 'self' blob:` from Phase 8) and are revoked on destroy; SVG logos are never inlined `[P8 draft]`.
- Sample fixtures (facts, briefs, beats, ZIP stats, listings summary, the Avery Sample profile) use fictional places and pass `scripts/guard-real-data.mjs` `[P4]`.
