Conventions (RESEARCH "Finance Definitions" — the contract; decisions recorded, MEDIUM-confidence choices called out):
- All amounts monthly integer cents; annual = monthly × 12 exactly.
- GSI = rent + other income. Vacancy % applies to GSI. EGI = GSI − vacancy.
- Management % applies to EGI ("collected income"). Maintenance, CapEx and custom % lines apply to rent. Tax/insurance/HOA/utilities are $ per month by default; if the user toggles one to %, it applies to rent.
- OpEx (excludes vacancy) = management + maintenance + capex + tax + insurance + HOA + utilities + custom. CapEx IS in OpEx (DealCheck convention; tooltip/definition says so).
- NOI = EGI − OpEx. Cash flow = NOI − P&I. Cap rate = NOI×12 ÷ price ×100. Cash invested = down + closing + repairs. CoC = cash flow×12 ÷ cash invested ×100. DSCR = NOI×12 ÷ (P&I×12) (NOI ÷ debt service flavor). GRM = price ÷ (rent×12) (rent only, before vacancy, excludes other income). 1% rule: pass ⇔ rent×100 ≥ price (integer compare). Rent-to-price % = rent ÷ price ×100.
- UI unit segments read "% of rent" / "$ per month" (UI-SPEC copy, verbatim); the computed "= $x a month" line shows the true dollars, so the vacancy (GSI) and management (EGI) bases are visible in results. This is the reconciliation between UI-SPEC copy and the RESEARCH reference cases; record it in the SUMMARY.
